Welcome

A quiet place to examine what is true

This site exists as a place for information, study, historical investigation, and thoughtful consideration concerning God, the elect, the righteous, the holy ones, beginnings, and the events understood through Scripture and other ancient writings.

The purpose is NOT to ask visitors to accept every statement simply because it appears here. Instead, visitors are encouraged to examine sources, compare historical evidence, read texts themselves, pray for wisdom and knowledge, and carefully consider what they encounter.

Seek understanding with humility. Examine what you hear. Pray for knowledge and wisdom.
