Purpose

Why this site exists

Truth in God of the Elect is intended to be a growing collection of information, research, documents, historical material, Scripture study, and personal examination concerning subjects related to God and the people described as the elect and righteous.

The site will explore subjects including the elect, the righteous, the holy ones, angels, judgment, ancient history, beginnings, and interpretations concerning the end times, including themes associated with Esau.

The material may draw upon the Tanakh and Torah, commonly referred to in Christian contexts as the Old Testament, as well as ancient writings such as 1 Enoch, Jasher, Jubilees, and other historical or religious sources.

Visitors are encouraged to distinguish between Scripture, historical evidence, interpretation, tradition, and personal belief.
